PLACE YOUR EARTHQUAKE CSV IN THIS FOLDER

Copy your dataset here and name it exactly:
  scraped_earthquake_data.csv

Required columns (aliases are supported):
  latitude, longitude, magnitude

Optional columns:
  time_utc (or time/date), depth_km (or depth), place/location, country

The app automatically loads /data/scraped_earthquake_data.csv at startup. There is no CSV upload button. Refresh the page after adding the file.

Note: files inside public/ are served by Vite. Do not put sensitive data here.
